# Menacing Ember Spitter (25 Nov)

This continues docs/art/menacing-foes (#801): Steve's ask for foes that look "more intentionally menacing and creature like", drawn by hand until the Meshy key for AI 3D models arrives.

The Ember Spitter (`drawSpitter` in client/textures.ts) was a round lump with a smiley-looking crack. Now it has:

- a row of four sharp, glowing vent spines in place of three stalks;
- hooked fangs round its burning mouth;
- slit eyes;
- jagged seams in place of the chevron crack.

The size, palette, throat sac and 4 frames are the same, so its "this one spits" read stays. No new meshes.

`sheet-before-after.png`: the sprite sheet at 3x, before on top and after below. Made with `KEYS=emberSpitter node tools/artsheet.cjs <out.png>`.
