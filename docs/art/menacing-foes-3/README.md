# Menacing foes, part 3: Ballast and Barge Hauler (25 Nov)

This continues docs/art/menacing-foes (#801), menacing-spitter (#804) and menacing-foes-2 (#818). These are drawn by hand in client/textures.ts until the Meshy key for AI 3D models arrives.

- **Ballast** (Foundry): the friendly round porthole is gone. It now has a narrow burning visor slit, a grille of iron teeth, and iron spikes on its helmet.
- **Barge Hauler** (Wrackline): the warm human face is now a drowned man's: grey-green and sunken, with a shadow under the hat brim, glowing slit eyes, a jaw of broken teeth, barnacles, and a grey-green hand.

The size, palette, frames and facing are the same, and there are no new meshes.

`sheet-before-after.png` shows before on top and after below. To re-make it, run `KEYS=ballast,bargeHauler node tools/artsheet.cjs <out.png>`.
