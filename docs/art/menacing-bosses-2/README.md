# Menacing bosses, part 2: Maelra, Grandmother Slag, Lensmother and the Furnace Regent (26 Nov)

Part 1 (docs/art/menacing-bosses) named these four as the next least menacing bosses: a round hood with a calm pale face, a round bug-like head, a friendly glowing lens, and a box head with a striped grille. All four are redrawn by hand in client/textures.ts with the part 1 helpers `spikes()`, `fangRow()` and `slitEyes()` (no paid AI tool before 1.0).

- **Maelra** (the Drowned Foundry): the round hood is now peaked with a torn, ragged rim. Inside it, a gaunt drowned skull with sunken sockets, cyan eyes slanted into a glare, and a gaping maw of needle teeth over the old riveted iron jaw. Barnacle spikes run along both shoulders and the iron hands have hooked claws.
- **Grandmother Slag** (the world boss shadow, the hub portrait and, through `drawSlagCroneRaid`, raid 2 in person): the round head is now a peaked hood over a hag's face in profile, with a hooked nose, slit ember eyes under a brow, a jutting chin and a crooked grin of fangs. Slag spurs jut along her hunched spine and bony talons grip the staff and the ladle. The raid's crown of slag spikes still sits along the hood.
- **Lensmother** (Glarewell): the great lens now has heavy lids closing into a V-shaped glare and a dark slit pupil in the burning iris. Brass thorns ray out between the halo lenses, long talons clutch the two hand lenses, and the veil hem is torn into ragged points.
- **The Furnace Regent** (the world boss shadow, the hub portrait and, through `drawRegentRaid`, raid 4 in person and his Abdication): the box head is now an iron helm tapering to a jaw, with two swept-back horns behind the flame crown, slit eyes glaring in a V and a furnace maw of iron fangs. Spiked pauldrons sit over both shoulders and iron talons curl round the poker.

Size, palette, eye colours, 4 frames, facing and the fight tells (Maelra's bellows and nozzle, the Crone's ladle and staff, the Lensmother's lenses and halo, the Regent's crown, grate, poker and Decree seals) are unchanged. Only canvas textures changed, so there are no new meshes: the fight shot measures 79 draw calls before and after (bar 96).

- `fight-before-after.png`: the four spawned round a Bulwark in a Foundry room, 3D at 320x568, before on the left, after on the right. Re-make it with `R=200 SPREAD=1.2 KINDS=maelra,grandmotherSlag,lensmother,regent node docs/art/menacing-bosses/shots.cjs dist <outDir> foundry <tag>` (the part 1 script, which now takes `R` and `SPREAD` for the ring; the raid bosses spawn at their 192 raid size). In 3D the Regent's small face is squashed by the camera tilt, so his horns and darker helm read more than the eyes.

No boss sheet is left with the old look among these: the raid sheets (slagCroneRaid, regentRaid, regentAbdicate, and colossusRaid/colossusMelt from part 1) all draw the base sheet first and add their extras on top, so they pick up the redraws. Kiln Mother, Vorhulm, Ysmeret and Nhevra already read as menacing.
