# "Was this made with AI?" Our answer (1.0, Checkpoint 1)

*Design, 27 Nov 2026. Pillar 5: one world, one voice, honestly made. Background: [roadmap.md](roadmap.md), "On 'AI slop', honestly", and [vision/research-market.md](vision/research-market.md) (player attitudes and store rules). For Steve to approve before anyone uses it in public (pillar 5: Steve approves every player-facing line).*

## The ground rules

1. **Never deny, never dodge.** The game is built by one person, Steve, working with Claude, an AI coding and writing assistant. We say so plainly when asked.
2. **Say only what is true on the day.** Each answer below has a "true when" line. If it isn't true yet, use the earlier answer.
3. **Name the difference that matters to players.** Players dislike generated art, music and voices most (Quantic Foundry survey, late 2025, in research-market.md). Our models, icons and sound effects are not generated by an image or audio model; they are built in code, piece by piece. That is the honest distinction, and it is worth saying.
4. **Don't call anything "hand-made" or "hand-written".** The code that builds the art and the text in the game were written with Claude. "Built in code" and "approved by Steve" are true; "hand-made" is not.

## The answers

### Short (store reply, comment, Discord), true today

> Yes, partly. Horders is made by one person working with Claude, an AI assistant, which helped write the code and the text. The 3D models, icons and sound effects aren't generated: they're built in code, piece by piece. The music was made with an AI music tool. Every name and line is checked by a person before it ships.

### Short, true once the music is replaced (if decision 2 is "replace")

> Partly. Horders is made by one person working with Claude, an AI assistant, which helped write the code and the text. There's no AI-generated art, music or voice: every model and sound is built in code, and the music is by a human composer. Every name and line is checked by a person before it ships.

*True when:* the Suno tracks are gone from the build, the new music's licence or contract is on file, and pillar 5's "no generated audio or images added" check has held since.

### Short, true if the music stays (if decision 2 is "keep and disclose")

Use the first answer, with the music sentence unchanged. Don't drop it.

### Longer (FAQ page, press, a review reply)

> **Who made Horders?** One person, Steve, with Claude, an AI assistant from Anthropic. Claude wrote most of the code and drafts the game's text; Steve sets the direction, plays every build and approves every name and line.
>
> **Is the art AI-generated?** No image or 3D generator made any of it. Every model, room, foe and icon is built from simple shapes in code, which is why it all shares one palette and one set of rules. *(Add once true: "and the music is by a human composer." Until then: "The music was made with an AI music tool.")*
>
> **Why say any of this?** Because you asked, and you should know what you're buying.

### What we never say

- "No AI was used." (False.)
- "Hand-made", "hand-crafted", "hand-written". (The code and text were written with Claude.)
- "AI-free art" without the music line while Suno tracks ship.
- Anything that blames or hides Claude ("just a tool we barely used").
