# Creative Director: debate response

*27 Nov 2026. I read all five proposals and both research reports.*

## Where we already agree

The five of us reached the same diagnosis on our own, which is the strongest evidence we have:

- Bring the camera in: heroes at 70 to 80 px.
- Cut the launch to places that each have their own creatures.
- Rename the draft to Sparks and hand-name every item.
- Replace Suno before we charge (85% of players felt negative about generative AI, according to Quantic Foundry).
- Put one button on the hub (P13).
- Make a loss pay and show mastery (P3, P7).
- Never sell power (P14).

We should stop debating these and build them.

## Ideas I now adopt

- **A visible enrage in place of Burnout (Combat).** It also fixes a story problem: you fall to the Kiln Mother, not to a timer, and she taunts you as she flares up.
- **The Art Director's "kiln-lit dioramas," in full.** A ceramic Court against an iron town turns my world into one rule for shape, material, colour and sound. The clay shatter beats anything I proposed.
- **Heirlooms with tempering (Systems)** in place of my 120 items. About 60 named Heirlooms plus a few plain items per place means less writing, and a duplicate drop still matters at hour 50. That avoids Archero's loot problem.
- **The event log and five cold testers per checkpoint (Product).** My checkpoints relied on gut feel. P15 says to measure.
- **A signature verb per class (Combat).** Overheat, Backstab and Parry also give each town trade its identity.

## Where I disagree

1. **Product's hook is a feature list, not a fantasy.** "Real boss fights in ten minutes" is accurate, but P2 finds that every lasting game has one sentence of fantasy ("escape the underworld"). The fantasy should carry the sentence. Portrait boss fights, the combination research §5 says no rival has, are the proof.
2. **The hub: town or card?** They fit together. The town is the backdrop behind the one Play button, and each building appears only when its system unlocks. That is P13 with a reason attached. I concede the cost: the slice ships one button over a town illustration, and the buildings come later.
3. **Systems adds too many new words at once.** Sparks, Fusions, Relics, Heirlooms, Blueprints, the Cache and the Descent each fold something in, but a new player meets six new words. Research names front-loading systems as an anti-pattern (Hades II, Archero 2). Ship Sparks and Fusions first. Hold Relics and Blueprints until human data shows that runs feel alike.
4. **Tiers 1 to 10, not 1 to 15.** I side with Systems over Combat here. P6 says difficulty should climb through rules, and five extra tiers of ×1.22 health are just numbers.
5. **The raid.** Product cuts every raid because lobbies could sit empty. That holds for four humans, but bots fill every role, and the Hoard is where the story ends. Ship it with bots and no matchmaking. If the bots can't carry it, it waits.

## Ideas that are wrong or too costly

- **My own 300 taunt lines.** I'm cutting them to about 100. Bad taunts read as AI filler, the very thing we're escaping.
- **Dropping the 2D mode** is right on cost, but it reverses a call Steve made, and the art redo takes 5 to 7 weeks. Gate it: nothing gets converted past one room until Steve picks the new look side by side with the old.
- **Kindled Resolve** is a patch. The real fix for melee is Combat's rule that every boss attack has a melee answer, plus the 60% floor.
- **Product's paywall at the Foundry door** works, but it should be the Court's first seal in the story, not a system popup.

## Three decisions the team must make together

1. **The name.** Keep "Horders" or rename it (Emberthief, Kilnward, Hearthless)? We have to decide before any store assets exist. "Horders" reads as a misspelling of "Hoarders" and suggests swarms.
2. **Launch scope.** Six, seven or eight places? Does the Hoard ship with bots? Ten tiers or fifteen? The answers set the size of the art, foe and writing work.
3. **The art and music bet.** The full diorama redo, no 2D mode, and code-made music, each gated by a one-room slice that Steve judges blind. If our music loses, we fund a composer.

## My vote

**Hook:** *Steal fire from a furnace of greedy fire-kings in short portrait boss fights you read and answer, and carry it home to relight your town.*

**Core loop: adjust.** Keep room-to-room delves, the auto-attack plus four buttons, and bosses that telegraph their attacks. Make these changes:

- 3 to 5 bigger foes per pack, built from foe jobs
- a flick-dodge
- an enrage in place of Burnout
- bosses at 60 to 90 seconds
- Sparks that change behaviour, plus Fusions
- every run carries embers home

**Art direction:** kiln-lit dioramas, with clay Court creatures against cast-iron heroes in a black void lit only by fire, and bright colour reserved for gameplay signals.

**Top 5 priorities:**

1. **One Kiln room that feels and looks final:** the camera, the hit-feel kit, the flick-dodge, the shatter and the diorama look.
2. **The world on paper:** the world bible, the voice guide, the name decision and the rename map.
3. **The Kiln Mother remade, with a loss that pays:** the enrage, melee answers, the mastery card, embers kept on a loss, and her taunts.
4. **The first 15 minutes:** a title screen, the opening, the one-button town hub, music in place of Suno, and the event log.
5. **Sparks with Fusions for all five classes**, and the launch places cut so that each has its own foes and a landmark room.
