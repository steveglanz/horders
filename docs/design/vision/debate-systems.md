# Debate: Systems and Retention Designer

## Where we already agree

All five proposals reached this list independently; lock it now:
- In-run picks become **Sparks**.
- Every item gets a hand-written name.
- The loss screen gets a lesson, a mastery line and progress (P3, P7).
- The hub chips are hidden and systems open one at a time (P13).
- We launch only the places that have their own foes.
- The Suno music is gone before we charge (85% of players negative; anti-pattern 8).
- We never sell power (P14).

## Where I disagree, and why

**The relit town (Creative) works as a stage but not as an economy.** One building per system best answers the eight chips; I adopt it as the shell for my unlock curve. The risk is that it becomes a second currency sink and a chore list, which is Hearthstone's lesson (anti-pattern 4). My proposal is that **buildings light at milestones** (a first boss kill, a Blueprint) and are never bought with embers. Creative's "keep your embers when you fall" and my "Blueprint shards on a loss" are two payouts for one problem. I would pick shards: they add run variety, not a stat sink.

**My Hoard Cache against Product's "Today" card with three tasks.** We share the goal (P12). The Cache fills from **wins alone**. Nothing like "play Lumen 3 times" pulls you off your class. It can live in the town's Notice Board. I adopt Product's daily-seed leaderboard for Quick Delve.

**Launch scope.** I asked for 8 places, Creative for 7 and Product for 6. **I now adopt Product's 6**:
- A four-player raid at launch risks empty lobbies (anti-pattern 11).
- The art cost table puts each extra boss at 1 to 2 days on top of its foes.
- The Hoard raid works better as **Season 1's finale**, a held-back gift like Path of Exile's fixed leagues.

**Tiers.** Combat wants tiers 1 to 15 with new rules past tier 6. I want tiers 1 to 10, then the Descent. Both are ladders of rules (P6). Two ladders mean two progress bars on the hub, so **(judgment)** we should have one.

**The art redo (5 to 7 weeks) is the biggest bet.** The research leans toward pixel art (P10). The Art Director's case is that agents build geometry better than they draw, and Thronefall backs that up. I support the redo, **but only after the one-room gate**. Dropping 2D reverses Steve's earlier call, so he has to approve it. Otherwise we polish a loop nobody has replayed.

## What I adopt from others

- **Combat's signature verbs become the base that Sparks and Fusions build on.** "Blink leaves a burning trail" lands harder once Overheat makes fire matter.
- **Combat's hard/easy room fork**, with the choice being a Blueprint or a Spark.
- **Combat's visible enrage** replaces Burnout. Kindled Resolve becomes optional.
- **Product's event log**, which is the biggest thing I missed. My checkpoint 1 was tested on bots only. Spark pick rate against win rate is how Slay the Spire balanced (P15).
- **Product's "no seasons at launch"** (Diablo IV; anti-patterns 2 and 3).
- **Creative's Court taunts**: cheap story progress through deaths, as in Hades.

## What I concede is too costly in my own proposal

I planned about 150 Sparks, 60 Heirlooms, 20 Fusions and 12 Omens, and Creative's 120 items and 300 lines come on top of that. At launch I now want **about 20 Sparks per class (8 that change behaviour), 2 Fusions per class, 40 Heirlooms and 8 Omens**.

## Ideas from others I think are wrong or premature

- **Renaming before the hook is proven.** "Horders" is a problem, but name it after the slice shows which fantasy testers repeat back.
- **An always-on world boss (Product).** It is a seventh system on the hub. Make it a season-finale weekend instead.
- **Fixing the paywall at the Foundry door before the unlock curve exists.** If the Cache, Blueprints and the tree all appear after minute 90, the free hour never shows why anyone would stay. Design both together.

## Three decisions we must make together

1. **Launch scope and the paywall line:** which 6 places ship, what is free, and which systems a player meets before the $5.99 offer.
2. **Loop first or look first:** whether Sparks, verbs and the feel kit are proven before the art redo scales up. Both share one Kiln slice room. Steve decides on dropping 2D.
3. **One meta-progression and one currency:** whether the hub is the town, a card or both, what embers buy, and which single payout a loss gives.

## My vote

- **Hook:** *Real boss fights you read and answer, in portrait, in ten minutes, as a fire thief robbing a furnace of greedy kings.*
- **Core loop: adjust.** Keep the room-to-room delve, the four buttons, the auto attack and the telegraphed bosses. Add:
  - 5 rooms with one fork, 6 to 8 minutes per delve
  - Sparks and Fusions built on each class's signature verb
  - one Relic per run
  - a flick-dodge
  - a visible enrage
  - a mastery end screen, where a loss always pays Blueprint progress
- **Art direction:** kiln-lit low-poly dioramas, with a clay Court and iron heroes. Fire is the only light, and only gameplay signals get flat colour.
- **Top 5 priorities:**
  1. **One Kiln room that feels great:** a 70 to 80 px hero, the hit-feel kit, the flick-dodge and the art slice.
  2. **Runs that differ:** verbs, Sparks with Fusions, one Relic, and the event log.
  3. **Losses that pay:** the mastery card, Blueprint shards, the enrage, and a win-rate floor for melee.
  4. **A world and a front door:** the world bible, hand-written names, and a one-button hub that opens one building at a time.
  5. **The first hour as a funnel:** the unlock curve and the paywall designed together, then tested on 5 cold players.
