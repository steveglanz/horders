# Debate: Combat and Feel Designer

*27 Nov 2026. I read all five proposals and both research reports.*

## Where we already agree

The five proposals agree more than they disagree, and that is good news.
- **Camera:** heroes at 70 to 80 px, trash at least 40 px, bosses at a third of the screen. Art, Creative, Product and I all name this. Market research gives the same numbers.
- **One look:** cut the 2D/3D mix.
- **Hand-written names** in place of prefix + slot.
- **Losses that pay,** with a mastery line ("dodged 9 of 11").
- **Bosses under 90 s** for every class.
- **A hub with one Play button.**
- **A smaller launch:** fewer places, each with its own foes.
- **Sparks** as the name for in-run picks.
- **Replace Suno** before we charge.

On these, debating any further wastes time. We should lock them.

## Where I disagree, and why

**1. Systems' first checkpoint builds content before the fight feels good.** Its C1 is Sparks, Fusions, Relics and Kindled Resolve, tested on today's 30 px heroes. P9 in the retention research calls juice "likely the best value for effort on the list." Dead Cells' combat praise is credited to "animation and sound design." A behaviour-changing Spark like "Blink leaves a burning trail" is worth little if you can't see the trail or feel the burn. Feel and camera go first. Sparks then get tuned on the real fight.

**2. Kindled Resolve is not a fix for melee.** Duskblade and Bulwark win 0 of 40 in Coldmarrow at tier 10 at average skill. Taking 10% less damage won't turn 0 wins into 24. The cause is boss attacks with no answer within melee reach (my change 5). Kindled Resolve is fine as an open accessibility aid. But if we ship it as the melee fix, it hides a design bug behind a mercy mechanic.

**3. Product's free melee class is Bulwark, the class that fails hardest late.** Free play is the Kiln at tiers 1 to 3, so the immediate risk is small. Still, the free class is the one people review. Bulwark should get its parry and pass the 60% floor before it becomes the face of melee.

**4. The tutorial teaches the wrong dodge.** Creative's opening teaches Blink as the answer to red. If every class gets the flick-dodge, the tutorial should teach the flick on red and leave E as the class verb. Otherwise four of the five classes learn a lesson that doesn't carry over.

**5. Delve length differs across three proposals.** Systems says 8 to 10 minutes, Product's pitch says ten, and I say 6 to 8 with 5 rooms. The median session is 5 to 6 minutes (GameAnalytics). Keeping the delve near that, plus a 4-minute Quick Delve, respects P11. Long delves also make a boss loss cost more, which is anti-pattern 7 ("losses that waste your time").

**6. Art's 30-degree field of view plus a closer camera hides threats.** I accept it only with my rules: edge arrows, and every telegraph either starts on screen or gives 1 s of warning.

**7. The systems mastery card still says "lost to Burnout".** Burnout goes away. A loss names the blow that killed you.

## Ideas I now adopt

- **Art's clay shatter as the kill burst** (6 to 10 shards that stay on the floor), and its **clay, iron, fire and steam sound materials** for the hit-feel kit. Quench as a steam hiss is the interrupt sound I wanted. On hit-stop, Art says 40 to 60 ms and I said 40 to 90. We settle it in the test scene.
- **Art's signal colours, each paired with a shape:** pink-red #FF2D6F with a serrated edge means move, a gold ring means stand here, and a cracked blue ring means interrupt. This beats my colour-only tells for colour-blind players.
- **Systems' Relic,** and tier steps of 3 and up as rules. This is my "difficulty from rules, not numbers" in their words. I'll make sure Relics such as "Heat never cools" stack with the Overheat verb on purpose, not by accident.
- **Product's event log, with cause of death.** It turns my fight budgets from bot estimates into human facts.
- **Creative's boss taunts,** used to announce the visible enrage ("You've stayed too long, little thief").

## Ideas I think are wrong or too costly

- **The content pile.** About 150 Sparks, 20 Fusions, 60 Heirlooms, 120 items and 300 lines, all before launch. Each Spark and Relic changes fight length and needs a bot rerun. Start with 2 Fusions per class, and grow only what the pick and win rates justify (P15).
- **Four separate "first slices".** Mine, Creative's, Art's and Product's are each one Kiln room. If we build them one after another, we lose about 6 weeks. Build **one** slice.
- **Eight places at launch** (Systems). My job roster needs 4 or 5 jobs plus a twist foe for each place. Six or seven places is what we can make distinct.

## Three decisions the team must make together

1. **One merged vertical slice, and what's in it.** My proposal: the Kiln entry room and the Kiln Mother in the clay-and-iron look; Emberwright plus one melee class; the feel kit and haptics; the flick-dodge; the visible enrage; the new end screen; the title screen and the opening; the event log live. Sparks and Relics come immediately after, not before.
2. **Delve and fight time budgets as a hard gate.** Trash 15 to 30 s, named fights 30 to 45 s, bosses 60 to 90 s, a 5-room delve of 6 to 8 minutes, and a 4-minute Quick Delve. Every system (Sparks, Relics, Omens, tiers) must fit inside these, or it doesn't merge.
3. **Launch scope.** Five solo layers plus the Hammerdeep (Product), plus the Hoard raid as the finale (Creative), or 8 places (Systems)? This sets how many distinct foe rosters, bosses and art builds we owe.

## My vote

- **Hook:** Readable boss duels in portrait, a few minutes each, where you dodge red, stand in gold and break blue casts, as a fire thief raiding the Furnace Court.
- **Core loop: keep and adjust.** Keep room-to-room delves, the stick, four buttons, auto-attack and telegraphed bosses. Add the flick-dodge, one signature verb per class, packs built from foe jobs, the fight budgets, a visible enrage instead of Burnout, Sparks that change behaviour, one Relic per run, and a loss that always pays.
- **Art direction:** Kiln-lit dioramas: flat-shaded low-poly clay foes and iron heroes, with fire as the only light and flat signal colours used only for gameplay.
- **Top 5 priorities:**
  1. One merged vertical slice: the camera, the hit-feel kit, the flick-dodge and the new look.
  2. Fight budgets, the visible enrage, the rule that every boss attack has a melee answer, and the 60% win-rate floor for every class.
  3. The first five minutes, the one-button hub, and an end screen that pays and teaches.
  4. Sparks that change behaviour, one Relic per run, and tier steps made of rules.
  5. Replace Suno, hand-name everything, and cut to the launch scope, with the event log running from the slice onward.
