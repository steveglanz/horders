Delete your Horders account

Horders, the game on horders.netlify.app and in the iPhone and Android apps. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Horders has no sign-up. Each place you play (a browser, a Home Screen app or the store app) gets its own anonymous account with a random id, which holds your cloud save and leaderboard times. You can delete it at any time from inside the game.

How to delete it

  1. Open Horders in the app, or at horders.netlify.app in the same browser you play in.
  2. Open Settings (the round button at the top right of the main screen).
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom, then tap again to confirm.

You need to be online. If you linked more than one place with a link code, repeat this in each place to remove each account.

What is deleted

  • The anonymous account itself
  • Your cloud save (your hero)
  • Your Delver's Keys and daily bounty leaderboard times
  • Your world boss record
  • Your link codes and links to other places
  • The hero and tips saved on that device

It happens right away and cannot be undone. Nothing is kept afterwards, except damage already dealt to the shared world boss, which holds no player id. Your display settings stay on the device until you remove the app or clear the browser's site data.

Only want a fresh start?

Settings > Start over resets one hero or all your progress and keeps the account.
