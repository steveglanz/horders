Horders privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026. This covers the game Horders on the website (horders.netlify.app) and in the iPhone and Android apps.

The short version

Horders has no sign-up. It never asks for your name, email address, age, phone number, location, contacts or photos. To keep your hero safe in the cloud, the game makes an anonymous account with a random id. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, no purchases, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

What is stored, and why

WhatWhyWhere
Your hero (level, gear, talents, embers, progress) and your settingsSo the game remembers youOn your device
An anonymous account: a random id and a sign-in tokenSo your hero can be saved online and found againOn your device and on our game server
A copy of your heroCloud save, so a cleared browser or a new install doesn't lose itGame server
Your best times on the Delver's Keys and daily bounty boards, shown with your class and level (for example "Emberwright 12")Public leaderboards that other players can seeGame server
Damage you dealt to the shared world bossThe shared world boss and its rewardsGame server
Link codes and which places share one hero"Play on another device" in Settings. A code stops working after 15 minutesGame server

The leaderboards show a class and level, never your name. Nothing else you type is stored except a link code you enter.

Playing together

When you play co-op, the game connects your device straight to the other players' devices (WebRTC). To set that up, a short handshake passes through our game server, and a public Google STUN server (stun.l.google.com) helps the devices find each other. Like any direct connection, this lets the devices in your party, and that STUN server, see each other's IP address. Game moves go device to device and are not stored.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase runs the game server: the anonymous accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, world boss and the co-op handshake.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google runs the STUN server used only when you start co-op.

Like any website or online service, these providers may keep technical logs of requests, such as IP addresses and times, to run and protect their services. We don't use them to identify you.

How long it is kept

Your hero and leaderboard times stay until you delete them. Link codes are removed once used or after they expire. Data on your device stays until you delete it in the game, clear the browser's site data, or remove the app.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings and tap Delete my account, then tap again to confirm. This deletes your anonymous account and everything tied to it on the game server (cloud save, leaderboard times, world boss record and link codes) and the hero on that device. It cannot be undone. More on the delete your account page.

If you only want a fresh hero, Settings > Start over resets your progress but keeps the account.

Children

Horders asks no one for personal information, children included. The only things stored are the game data listed above.

Changes

If what the game stores changes, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
