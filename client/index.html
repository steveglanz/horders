How to playEach delve is a short dungeon: four tough fights, then its boss. Move with WASD or drag on a touch screen; your basic attack fires on its own. Spend your class's Heat, Momentum, Resolve or Radiance on your action bar (Q, E, R, F or the thumb buttons), dodge the red, and interrupt with F when an enemy cast bar turns blue. Every fight cleared is a level, a talent and a loot bag, and your hero keeps the gear.
